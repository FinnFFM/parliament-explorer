# Credits

Maintainer: Finn Karstens.

This application reworks the corpus-browsing ideas from Reden-Portal and Multimodal Parliament Explorer. Contributors to the earlier collaborative application were **Emre Es, Nico Irani, Finn Karstens and Julia Jeschke**. Their contribution to that foundation is acknowledged here; the new local SQLite interface does not claim to preserve every feature of that application.

Third-party runtime libraries are SQLite JDBC (Xerial), Jackson and SLF4J. Their respective licenses and notices apply. The frontend uses browser APIs and system fonts, without third-party scripts.

All bundled example speakers, groups and speeches are fictional. No third-party video, portrait, transcript collection or database credential is included.
